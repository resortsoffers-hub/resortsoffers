## 2. LinkedIn – Nora (personal) · Four Seasons 2027 openings  ➜ post in 2 days

Four Seasons has shared 13 destinations to watch in 2027, and the list says a lot about where luxury travel is heading.

A few that stand out to me:
📍 Madinah and Jeddah, Saudi Arabia: refined hospitality close to the Prophet's Mosque and on the Red Sea corniche
📍 Zanzibar: a beachfront retreat designed to complement safari experiences in Tanzania
📍 Luxor and Cairo, Egypt: luxury as a gateway to heritage
📍 Berlin and Gstaad: historic landmarks given a new chapter
📍 Okinawa, Japan: architecture by Kengo Kuma

The common thread: luxury is no longer only about the room. It's about place, culture and a story guests can't find anywhere else.

Which of these destinations would you visit first?
