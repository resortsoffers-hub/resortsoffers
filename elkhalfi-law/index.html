FAQ

Common questions

Is my first consultation confidential?

Yes. Everything you share with us during a consultation is kept strictly confidential.

Do I have to go to court?

Not always. Many family matters are resolved through negotiation or mediation. We will help you choose the approach that fits your situation, and represent you in court if it becomes necessary.

How long does a divorce take?

It depends on whether the divorce is contested, the complexity of finances, and whether children are involved. We will give you a realistic timeline after reviewing your case.

How are your fees structured?

We discuss fees openly at the start. Depending on the matter, we offer fixed fees or hourly billing, with no surprises along the way.

What should I bring to my first meeting?

Any court documents, marriage or birth certificates, and a summary of income, assets, and debts. Don't worry if you don't have everything; we will guide you.
