Bliss Hotel Seychelles — Mahé

Boutique cliffside hotel at Glacis, minutes from Beau Vallon, with ocean-view rooms and a seawater pool on the rocks.

Deluxe resortHoneymooners

Overview

Bliss Hotel sits on the rocks at Glacis on the north-west coast of Mahé, a short drive from Beau Vallon and Victoria.

The hotel is small and personal, with sea-facing rooms, a rock pool and direct ocean access — a good-value 4-star choice for couples.

Rates for this property are quoted on request. Send us your dates, room category and party size and we'll come back with a full quotation including transfers.
