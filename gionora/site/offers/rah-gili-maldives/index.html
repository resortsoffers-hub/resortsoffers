Overview

Rah Gili sits on a classic Maldivian island of white sand and turquoise lagoon, reached by a short luxury speedboat transfer.

The offer covers a 3-night stay on Half Board with return speedboat transfers and Green Tax for base occupancy — with generously sized pool villas on the beach, over the water, and in two-bedroom family layouts.

Highlights

  • Return luxury speedboat transfers included
  • Beach and over-water pool villas — several with a water slide
  • Two-bedroom family villas for larger parties
  • Half Board included, upgrade to Full Board or All Inclusive
