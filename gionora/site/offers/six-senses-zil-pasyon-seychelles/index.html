Overview

Six Senses Zil Pasyon occupies Félicité Island, a granite island of hidden coves ten minutes by boat from La Digue.

Villas and residences are spread across the island for total privacy, with Six Senses wellness, sustainability and family programmes throughout.

Rates for this property are quoted on request. Send us your dates, room category and party size and we'll come back with a full quotation including transfers.

Highlights

  • Transfer: Domestic flight to Praslin (15 minutes) plus a 20-minute boat, or helicopter transfer from Mahé
  • 5-star · Félicité Island
  • Whole private island of Félicité — boulders, coves and empty beaches
  • Thirty pool villas plus multi-bedroom residences
  • Six Senses Spa built among the granite rocks
  • Grow With Six Senses kids club and Earth Lab sustainability programme
