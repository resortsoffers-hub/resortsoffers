## PRESS RELEASE BEST PRACTICES:

1. **Timing:** Send Tuesday-Thursday, 10 AM local time
2. **Subject Line:** "PRESS RELEASE: [Compelling Headline]"
3. **Email Format:** Plain text + PDF attachment
4. **Follow-up:** Call 24-48 hours after sending
5. **Multimedia:** Include high-res images, logos, videos
6. **SEO:** Include relevant keywords naturally
7. **Links:** Add 2-3 relevant website links
8. **Quotes:** Include 1-2 quotes from company executives
9. **Length:** 400-600 words ideal
10. **Boilerplate:** Keep company description consistent
